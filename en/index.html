About

I left school with a basic vocational qualification, no Abitur — real-world problems taught me more than another year of exams would have. Training as an IT specialist for application development was the actual start; from there it was several years as a consultant, then Managing Director & CTO at MOBIKO, where I've scaled systems, teams and the company itself through several growth phases.

My actual focus isn't technology alone, though — it's the translation between tech and business: understanding how a technical decision plays out in growth, margin and capital efficiency, and vice versa, how a capital decision plays out technically.

Germany's transport sector produced roughly 146 million tonnes of CO2 in 2020 — the third-largest emitter after energy and industry. With MOBIKO's flexible, tax-optimized mobility budgets, I work on changing that instead of just talking about it.

As a part-time investor, I regularly sit on the other side of the table and see how investors actually evaluate companies. As a mentor, I pass that on to founders and tech leaders facing exactly these scaling questions.

Off-screen: gravel biking, bouldering, cooking for fun — and a dog I'm more attached to than most frameworks.
