About

I'm Managing Director & CTO at MOBIKO, where I've scaled systems, teams and the company itself through several growth phases. My actual focus isn't technology alone, though — it's the translation between tech and business: understanding how a technical decision plays out in growth, margin and capital efficiency, and vice versa, how a capital decision plays out technically.

As a part-time investor, I regularly sit on the other side of the table and see how investors actually evaluate companies. As a mentor, I pass that on to founders and tech leaders facing exactly these scaling questions.
